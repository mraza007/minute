Minute vs Otter

An Otter alternative with no bot and no minute limits

Otter's assistant joins your Zoom, Teams, and Google Meet calls, and its free Basic plan includes 300 transcription minutes a month. Minute is a free Otter alternative. It records on your Mac, no bot joins the call, there is no monthly quota, and transcription runs offline.

No bot in your call

Minute does not join your meeting. It records your microphone. On macOS 13 or later, it can also capture system audio, which is how it hears the other people on the call. System audio needs the Screen Recording permission. Nobody on the call sees a new participant.

Turn on meeting detection and Minute offers to record when a call starts in Zoom, Teams, Webex, Slack, FaceTime, Discord, or a browser, Google Meet included. It checks which apps use the microphone. It does not listen to do this.

No minutes to run out of

Minute has no account and no plan, so it has no monthly quota and no limit per meeting. Whisper transcribes on your Mac in 99 languages. When the recording stops, a local language model writes the summary, the decisions, and the action items. Ask answers questions about the meeting and links each answer to the moment it was said.

There is one limit. If a meeting is too long for the summary model, the summary says that it covers only part of the recording.

When Otter is the better choice

Minute is not the right Otter AI alternative for everyone. Otter fits better in three cases.

You want notes written and shared for you.

Otter's assistant joins Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet calls to do this. Minute keeps each note on your Mac, and you share it yourself.

You need to transcribe recordings you already have.

Otter's Basic plan includes three file imports. Minute has no file import.

You do not use a Mac.

Minute runs only on macOS 11 or later. Apple silicon is the tested target, and the Intel build is not yet tested on real Intel hardware.

Keep your meetings on your Mac.

Minute is the free, offline AI note taker for macOS.
