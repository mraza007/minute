02 What it does
Live transcription

Transcribed as you speak, every speaker labeled.

On-device Whisper keeps pace with the conversation, and fully local diarization works out who said what — so the transcript reads like a conversation, not a wall of text.

Ask your notes

Answers with receipts.

Ask any meeting a question and get an answer with timestamp citations that jump to the moment it was said. Computed locally, like everything else.

Meeting detection

A call starts. One click and it's on the record.

When Zoom, Teams, or a browser call starts using your mic, a quiet pill appears — one click starts recording. It watches device state, never audio, and it's off by default.

Your hardware, your models

Pick the brains.

Choose your Whisper size and summary model with honest recommendations for your Mac's memory. Metal-accelerated, offline after the download.

Dark mode

Ink on dark paper.

Follows your Mac's appearance — warm, quiet, and readable at 11 pm. Dark mode included. Cloud not.

03 Under the hood

Native, not wrapped.

No Electron, no Python runtime, no Docker sidecar. Minute is a Rust app with whisper.cpp and llama.cpp compiled in, running on Metal. It's signed and notarized by Apple, updates itself from GitHub releases, and stores everything in files you own.
